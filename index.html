Echoes of Clay: India's Forgotten Hands

Brand campaign concept

A 3-phase digital revival campaign for India's indigenous pottery traditions — storytelling, AR filters, pop-up "clay cafes" and all.

Read the concept
Tagline: "India was never built in cities. It was shaped in clay." Phase 1 "Mud Has a Voice": a raw, unscripted docu-series on potters — no polish, just hands in motion. Phase 2 "Craft Your Clay": a mobile app to digitally mold clay, plus an AR pottery filter. Phase 3 "Own the Earth": pop-up clay cafes in metro cities — tea in kulhads, no WiFi, just mud and music. Goal: a sustainable e-commerce platform run by potters, for potters.
